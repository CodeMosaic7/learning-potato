# 03 - Student Personas & Learning Needs

## Overview

This document establishes provisional student personas to guide V1 product design and adaptation policies.

> [!NOTE]
> **Design Artifact Notice**: These personas are product design tools based on the confirmed platform mission for underprivileged children. They do not represent formal demographic user research.

---

## Student Persona Profiles

### Persona 1: "Aarav" — The Struggling Foundational Learner
- **Background**: 10 years old, 5th Grade. Accesses platform on a shared low-end smartphone over 3G.
- **Cognitive & Academic Profile**:
  - Reading Level: Below Grade Level (3rd Grade vocabulary).
  - Comprehension & Reasoning: Needs concrete real-world examples; struggles with multi-step abstract instructions.
  - Confidence: Low self-efficacy; easily discouraged by formal test jargon.
- **Key System Needs**:
  - Simple, encouraging vocabulary in Chatbot responses.
  - Step-by-step micro-guidance in Homework Helper.
  - Quizzes that start with foundational difficulty and offer immediate hints.

### Persona 2: "Priya" — The Curious & Fast-Paced Learner
- **Background**: 13 years old, 8th Grade. Accesses platform at a local community center PC.
- **Cognitive & Academic Profile**:
  - Reading Level: Grade Level or Above.
  - Comprehension & Processing Speed: Fast processing pace; eager for multi-step reasoning challenges.
  - Interaction Preference: Concise summaries over lengthy conversational warm-ups.
- **Key System Needs**:
  - Dynamic challenge questions in Quizzes.
  - Accelerated learning roadmap progression.
  - Direct, structured explanations with optional advanced concept expansion.

### Persona 3: "Rohan" — The Visual & Practical Learner
- **Background**: 12 years old, 7th Grade. Uses an older tablet.
- **Cognitive & Academic Profile**:
  - Learning Preference: Strong visual and analogy preference; struggles with dense text blocks.
  - Attention Consistency: Shorter attention bursts; responds well to interactive gamification (XP, badges, streaks).
- **Key System Needs**:
  - Bulleted, visually formatted Chatbot outputs with real-life analogies.
  - Visual mastery indicators and streak tracking on Personalised Dashboard.

---

## Summary of Learner Need Variations Map

```text
Reading Ability      : Below Grade Level  <--------------------->  Above Grade Level
Explanation Depth    : Micro-Steps & Hints <--------------------->  Concise Concept Summary
Quiz Difficulty      : Foundational Scaffolding <--------------->  Advanced Challenge
Learning Pace        : Frequent Revision <----------------------->  Accelerated Sequence
```
